# SaffScreen Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 5 September 2026**

SaffScreen – Smart Masjid Display ("SaffScreen", "the App") is developed by **I.N.U. Computers, Bikaner, Rajasthan, India**. This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when used on Android TV or Windows.

## 1. Information the App accesses

### Location

SaffScreen may request approximate and precise device location only when the user chooses to obtain the device's current latitude and longitude. This information is used to calculate local prayer times.

Location is processed on the device and saved locally in the App's settings. SaffScreen does not transmit the device's latitude or longitude to I.N.U. Computers, advertising services, analytics providers, or other external servers. Users may deny location permission and enter latitude and longitude manually.

### Mosque and display settings

Mosque names, prayer and congregation times, announcements, language, theme, font, Ramadan settings, Taraweeh progress, and other display preferences are stored locally on the device.

### Administrator credentials

The administrator username and salted password hashes are stored locally. Plain-text administrator and recovery passwords are not stored. A secure local token may be used to remember a trusted device.

## 2. Mobile remote connection

SaffScreen can provide a local web remote while the TV and the user's phone are connected to the same local Wi-Fi network. Remote settings are transferred directly between the phone's browser and the SaffScreen device over that local network. SaffScreen does not operate a remote cloud account or upload these settings to I.N.U. Computers.

Users should use this feature only on a trusted private network and should protect their administrator credentials.

## 3. Internet and Hadith content

The App connects over HTTPS to a public GitHub-hosted content file to download verified Hadith records and stores a local cache for offline display. The App does not attach the user's prayer settings, location coordinates, administrator credentials, or mosque information to this request.

As with ordinary internet connections, GitHub may receive technical connection information such as an IP address, request time, and browser or device network information. GitHub processes that information under its own privacy terms:

https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## 4. Data collection, advertising, and sharing

SaffScreen:

- does not require users to create an online account;
- does not include advertising;
- does not use analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs;
- does not sell personal information;
- does not share personal information for advertising or marketing;
- does not upload locally stored mosque settings, prayer settings, announcements, location coordinates, or administrator credentials to I.N.U. Computers.

## 5. Data storage, retention, and deletion

App settings, credentials, trusted-device tokens, Taraweeh progress, and cached Hadith content remain on the user's device until changed, reset, or removed.

Users can delete locally stored data by using available reset controls or by clearing the App's storage/uninstalling the App. Because SaffScreen does not provide a cloud user account or store this local data on an I.N.U. Computers server, there is no separate cloud account to delete.

## 6. Permissions

Depending on the device and selected features, SaffScreen may request:

- **Location permissions:** to obtain latitude and longitude for local prayer-time calculation;
- **Internet and network-state access:** to download verified Hadith content and provide network-dependent features;
- **Receive boot completed:** to support the optional Android TV auto-start feature.

Permissions are used only for the functions described above.

## 7. Security

SaffScreen uses reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS for cloud content, salted password hashing, secure local token storage where supported, and local-only storage for mosque configuration. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 8. Children's privacy

SaffScreen is a general mosque display utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SaffScreen's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, contact:

**I.N.U. Computers, Bikaner**  
Email: **inucomputersbikaner@gmail.com**
